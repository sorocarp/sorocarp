      The rules it follows
      1. Each particle senses the trail at three points ahead of it and turns toward the strongest reading.
      2. A hungry particle also smells food, whose scent is the token's score, and settles on it to eat. A fed one ignores scent and wanders along trails.
      3. It moves one cell and lays trail where it lands. Trail diffuses and evaporates every step. Full cells block the way.
      4. Food in a cell is shared, so a node can only carry so much body. Weak food refills particles slowly.
      5. Enough energy and it divides into a free neighbouring cell. No energy and it dies.
      6. Toxic food (negative score) has no scent and drains energy. Branches toward it starve and the trail evaporates.
