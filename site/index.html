How it works

The whole idea, in three steps.

  1. 01

    Tokens become food

    Every token gets one score from its price action, volume and liquidity. A good score makes it food with a strong scent. A bad score makes it poison.

  2. 02

    The organism grows toward it

    Thousands of particles follow the scent, eat, and multiply. Good food gets a thick tube. Weak food gets a thin one. Anything toxic starves and the branch disappears.

  3. 03

    Its body is the position

    The share of the body sitting on each token is how much of that token it holds. No model picks trades. The allocation is just where the organism ended up.

No planner. No optimiser. No AI picking trades. Seven simple rules, a few thousand particles, and a market that keeps moving the food.

      The rules

      Seven rules. That is the entire mind.

      Real slime mould solves mazes and rebuilds rail networks without a single neuron. This is a particle model of the same behaviour. Every particle follows these, every step.

      1. 01

        Hunger

        A hungry particle can smell food. A fed one cannot, so it wanders and explores instead.

      2. 02

        Settle

        A hungry particle standing on food tends to stay and eat.

      3. 03

        Sense

        It reads the trail ahead of it at three points: front, left and right.

      4. 04

        Turn

        It turns toward the strongest reading. That is the whole decision.

      5. 05

        Move

        One step forward. A crowded cell blocks the way, which is why the body spreads out.

      6. 06

        Leave a trail

        It marks where it has been. Trail fades, so only busy paths stay.

      7. 07

        Feed, divide, die

        Food gives energy, poison takes it. Enough energy and it splits in two. None and it dies.

      What counts as food

      The organism never sees a price. One small formula turns each token into a number between minus one and one, and that number is all it can taste.

      Questions

      Plain answers.

      What is this?

      A slime mould with a portfolio, running as a few thousand particles in your browser. Tokens are placed around it as food. It grows toward the ones that score well and abandons the ones that turn bad. The share of its body on each token is its position in that token.

      Is it trading real money?

      No. It is paper trading. The positions are the organism's live allocation, tracked against a synthetic market feed, and no real funds move. The Solana slot shown on the page is a genuine live read of the chain. If on-chain execution is added, the treasury address and every transaction will be published here so anyone can check the book against the chain.

      Is an AI deciding what to buy?

      No. Every position comes from the seven particle rules: scent, crowding, feeding and starvation. The narrator and the backrooms are written by a language model, but they only describe what the organism already did. They can watch. They cannot steer.

      Why is it still on a token that turned toxic?

      Because it is an organism, not a switch. Particles on a toxic token starve over a few seconds and the trail leading there fades. The weight drains away as the branch dies.

      Why does it not just put everything on the best token?

      Food is shared. A token can only feed so much body, so extra particles go hungry and wander off to find something else. That is how it ends up spread across several tokens without anyone telling it to diversify.
